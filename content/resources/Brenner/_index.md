In November 2004 the British SWP held a dayschool under the title "Capitalism". It was organised in 3 sessions:

* The Transition from Feudalism to Capitalism

* Capitalism Today

* Imperialism

In each session there were two main speakers: Robert Brenner and a speaker from the SWP. In the Transition session the SWP speaker was Chris Harman, in the Capitalism session it was Rob Hoveman, and in the Imperialism session Alex Callinicos.
