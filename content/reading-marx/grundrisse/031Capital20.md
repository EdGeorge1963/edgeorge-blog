Marx turns his attention to the notion of overproduction (the “general glut”).

It is “nonsense” to assert that overproduction is impossible. To say this is to assert in effect the identity of the process of the production of capital with its process of valorisation (“*Verwertungsprocess*”). In its least “sophistical” (i.e. most direct) form, this amounts to the proposition that “supply = its own demand”.
