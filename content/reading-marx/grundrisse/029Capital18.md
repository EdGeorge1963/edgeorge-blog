### (1) Valorisation and realisation

We have seen, says Marx, that capital is exchanged for labour, through which it maintains its value, and then augments this value, in production, through the production of a surplus value. By means of the unity of process of valorisation with process of production there emerges a product—capital—which is a value of a greater magnitude than that which acted as point of departure.
