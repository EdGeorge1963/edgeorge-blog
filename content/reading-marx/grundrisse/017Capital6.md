### [(1) The exchange of labour against capital]

In the exchange between the worker and the capitalist the former obtains money and the latter a commodity---a use value---whose price is equal to the money exchanged for it; from the worker (the seller)’s perspective, what the buyer in the exchange does with what she buys has no bearing on the economic relationship.
