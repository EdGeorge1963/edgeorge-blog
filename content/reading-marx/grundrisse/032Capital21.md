Marx revisits one of the iterations of the previous numerical example.

A capitalist produces cotton twist (yarn), producing 40 lb in total. The “real value” of the twist is 5 thalers a pound, a quantity which we can break down into 4 1/2 thalers production cost (materials, instrument, wages, etc.) and 12 profit, which gives a rate of profit of (1/2)/(4 1/2) = 11 1/9 %.
