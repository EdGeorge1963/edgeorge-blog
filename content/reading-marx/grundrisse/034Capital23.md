Imagine five capitalists: A, B, C, D and E. Capitalist E produces only means of subsistence consumed by workers. Even though she would realise her profit through exchange with wages, “his profit would originate not in the exchange of his commodity for the workers’ money, but in the exchange of his capital with living labour.” It is not in the sale of the product that profit arises, but in the purchase of the living labour that operates in *production*.
